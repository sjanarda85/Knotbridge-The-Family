# Knotbridge – The Family

**Knotbright Comics** graphic novel series.

A lawyer by day. A doctor by night. Two brilliant minds. Two impossible gifts. When the system turns its back on the helpless, they become the last line of defense — and raise the next generation of heroes.

## Issues (10 pages each)

| Issue | Title |
|-------|-------|
| #1 | The Silent Advocates |
| #2 | Bloodline Awakening |
| #3 | Family Protocol |

## Characters

- **Marcus Hale / The Advocate** – Lawyer. Hypercognition + golden oath-chains.
- **Dr. Sophia Hale / Mercy** – Doctor. Bio-kinetic healing & disruption.
- **Leo Hale (11)** – Kinetic redirection.
- **Mia Hale (9)** – Light manipulation (invisibility, hard-light).
